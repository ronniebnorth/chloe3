## Not yet confirmed by ear

Ask about these:

1. **Does the 3rd land as a release?** Did the wait feel like waiting, or like nothing happening?
   At 15 minutes the wait is about 7 minutes. This is the point of the whole journey.
2. Melody mode makes the first chord after the drop land on a random note, not always the root. If
   the drop feels weak, make it land on the root.
3. Does the jet read as a jet, or as wind or hiss? Is the −45 dB faint jet audible at all?
4. The mix rises about 10 dB in under 30 s through the pass. Gentle enough?
5. Is one key change every 2 minutes too often? Does Rise still drive in melody mode?
6. Draw: do broad shapes come through clearly?

## Testing setup

- Headless Chrome with CDP via `ws` in the scratchpad (`npm i ws`); `vite preview`. Audible by his
  choice: say "playing now" before and report when it's stopped. He may be listening: use
  `--mute-audio` if so; analysers still work.

  It makes levels near a forced swap look wrong, so check levels at real speed.
- `AudioBufferSourceNode` has its own `start`, separate from `AudioScheduledSourceNode.prototype.start`.
- Don't `pkill -f` a pattern in your own command line: it kills the shell (exit 144).
