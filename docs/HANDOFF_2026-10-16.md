## Symphonic architecture (src/App.jsx)

- **Samples:** `smplr@1.1.0`, MusyngKite soundfonts from gleitz.github.io (~3 MB each): string_ensemble_1, contrabass, cello, timpani, taiko_drum, violin, flute; brass_section, tuba, orchestra_hit load afterwards in the background. `getOrchestra(ac)`.
- **Gotcha:** MusyngKite string samples are only **3.16 s and stop dead**, with no loop data on goldst.dev, so chords are **re-bowed** every 2.2 s. Contrabass has loop data (`loadLoopData`, `loop: true`).
- **Routing:** bus 0.65 → dry 0.3 + own cathedral convolver `makeHall` (RT60 6 s, 50 ms pre-delay, darkening tail), wet 1.4 × REV. Strings have their own `swell` gain (a hairpin per chord). Chloe's synth line goes through a `lead` bus (1.6 kHz low-pass, gain 0.8, 0.5 send to the hall) when the orchestra is on.
- **`orchTick`** (inside the playback effect): every 1–4 bars it picks a chord by scale-degree 4ths/5ths, then plays strings, bass, cello ostinato, timpani/taiko and violin/flute licks. Uses `lastBase` (blend-aware notes) and the current tuning via `detune`.
- **Tension → energy:** `tensionOf()` gives the average pair clash, mapped 0.20 → 0 to 0.33 → 1, plus 0.4 while a blend is morphing. Energy rises fast and falls slowly. It drives melody 16ths/8ths, cello speed, chord length, lick and drum density, and a **resolve** (home chord + boom + swell) after a peak. `energyRef` is shared with Auto.
- **March:** `playMarch()`, 2–4 bars of brass stabs, cello + tuba 8th chug, hits, timpani/taiko, and a final crunch with ♭2 if the scale has one. ≥45 s apart; 35% when energy > 0.6, else 7% per chord. Built in the style of a film villain march, **not** the Williams melody (copyright).
